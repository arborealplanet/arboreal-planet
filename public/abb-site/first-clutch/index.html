The first season

Some stories change everything that comes after.

In 2023, a Sorong sire and Lereh dam produced my first Green Tree Python clutch. These photos follow the pairing and hatch, then introduce the individuals documented in the original project archive.

01 / The pairing

Two animals, one first attempt.

The Sorong sire came from a local arboreal keeper in 2020. I acquired the Lereh dam in 2018 at the reptile expo in Daytona. I paired them in March 2023, and they remained together through September.

02 / The eggs

October 13, 2023.

The dam laid ten white eggs. It was the first clutch I had produced, and the start of a new part of the work: watching and documenting each stage rather than only the finished animal.

03 / The hatch

Two days. Ten yellow neonates.

All ten hatched between December 2 and 3. Every neonate emerged yellow. The first glimpse of a hatchling breaking through an egg remains one of the defining images of this project.
