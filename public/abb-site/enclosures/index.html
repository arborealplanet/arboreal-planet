Quick answers

Enclosure questions.

What is included in an enclosure?

Each listed model includes its bin, the perch layout described in its card, and a water branch with an 8 oz deli cup. Shipping and heat pads are not included in the listed price.

What changes between Adult and Breeder?

Adult uses a 64 qt bin and three 19 in PVC perches. Breeder uses a 106 qt bin and three 29 in PVC perches. Both use ¾ in PVC for the tri-perch system.

Can I ask about a perch or water branch separately?

Yes. The parts section lists the available types. Ask for current pricing and specify the size or model you need.

What comes with the Neonate Growth Kit?

A Neonate enclosure and a Sub-Adult top perch. Ask for the current kit price before ordering.
