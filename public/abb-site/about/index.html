Arboreals By Bunn.

I'm Gage Bunn. After more than fifteen years working with reptiles, Green Tree Pythons became the center of this work: the animals, the records behind them, and the enclosures I use every day.

Where it began

Built from keeping, not a catalog.

My first Green Tree Python clutch came in 2023. Since then, documenting individual animals and refining the spaces they live in have gone hand in hand. Read the first-clutch story →

Arboreals By Bunn is where those projects live. Chondro Dojo is the enclosure line shaped by daily use: perch arrangements, water access, and layouts that are straightforward to maintain.

Locality and lineage matter, but a label alone never tells the whole story. I share the context available for an animal and leave room for uncertainty when its history is incomplete.
